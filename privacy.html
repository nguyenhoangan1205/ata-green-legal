Privacy Policy

ATA Green Poster · Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ATA Green ("we", "us", "our"), based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, collects, uses and protects information when you use ATA Green Poster (the "App"), our internal tool for publishing our own real estate content to TikTok.

1. Information we collect

When you connect a TikTok account and grant permission, we receive only the data allowed by the scopes you approve:

TikTok scopeDataPurpose
user.info.basicOpen ID, display name, avatarIdentify which account is connected
video.listList of your public videos and their basic metadataShow what has been published and avoid duplicates
video.upload / video.publishVideos and photos you choose to upload, captionsPublish content to your TikTok account at your request

We also store OAuth access tokens needed to keep the connection active. We do not collect your TikTok password.

2. How we use information

  • To publish content to your TikTok account when you request it.
  • To display basic account and video information inside the App.
  • To maintain, secure and troubleshoot the App.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except as described below.

3. Sharing

  • TikTok – content you publish is sent to TikTok and is governed by TikTok's privacy policy.
  • Service providers – we use integration providers (such as Composio) to securely store tokens and make API calls on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements – we may disclose information if required by law.

4. Data retention

We keep access tokens and account information only while your TikTok account remains connected. When you disconnect the App or ask us to delete your data, we delete the stored tokens and associated information within 30 days.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to authorized staff, to protect your information.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing us.

7. Children

The App is intended for business use by adults and is not directed to children under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision.

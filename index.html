ATA Green Poster

by ATA Green · Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

ATA Green Poster is an internal content publishing tool used by ATA Green, a real estate rental business in Ho Chi Minh City. It helps our team publish our own rental property videos and photos to the TikTok accounts we own and manage, from one place.

How it works

  1. An authorized ATA Green staff member signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and grants permission.
  2. We prepare a rental listing video or photo set with a caption.
  3. The App uploads the content through the TikTok Content Posting API, and the staff member reviews and publishes it to our TikTok account.
  4. The App shows basic account info and the list of our published videos so we can track what has been posted.

Data and permissions

The App only requests the TikTok permissions it needs (user.info.basic, video.list, video.upload, video.publish) and never collects TikTok passwords. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
